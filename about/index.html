I'm Tae Hyun Kim, and most people call me Eric. I studied Interactive Media at the University of York, then Design and Digital Media at the University of Edinburgh, where I was also the programme representative for my year.

Between the two degrees I served in the Republic of Korea Army as a public affairs specialist, making training and promotional videos from first idea to final edit. Watching how people reacted to each video, then changing the next one, is still how I like to work.

I'm drawn to the small things people notice without knowing why: an icon nobody can decode, a number that doesn't add up, a request that feels awkward to send. My case studies show what I found, what I changed, and what I'd still fix.
