Graduate product designer with an MSc in Design and Digital Media from the University of Edinburgh. Based in Seoul and ready to relocate.

BudgetBills2024

A personal finance app centred on bill splitting, designed to remove the social friction of asking for money.

In a survey of 40 respondents, 47.5% cited talking about money as a challenge when splitting bills, yet only 20% reported regular disputes.

Information architecture, usability testing, Figma prototype

Thryve2025

A gamified health app for users who don't habitually track their health, developed as a working React Native build for my MSc thesis.

Design feedback described the first badge set as militaristic, at odds with the brand's playful principles. I redesigned the system around a robot character, modelled in Blender, that evolves with user progress.

User research, brand, UX/UI, 3D modelling, React Native

Ignis Oasis2024

Visual identity, icon set, poster campaign and a 22-second motion infographic for a speculative climate protest organisation.

Print testing showed the centre poster's RGB lighting effects didn't reproduce in CMYK, so I adapted the design for print production.

Visual identity, posters, motion graphics, guidelines
